What the project is trying to build

The goal is not to make a blockchain substitute for Christianity. The goal is to build durable, transparent and privacy-conscious tools that can carry Christian content, prayer evidence, fellowship and service across platforms while keeping user agency intact.

Mission before mechanics

Christ centered

Every feature should direct attention toward Jesus Christ rather than speculation, personalities or technological novelty.

Safety and human dignity

Core prayer, Scripture and safety resources should not depend on wealth, token ownership or public self-identification.

Non-custodial participation

Users review and authorize wallet actions through Xaman. JCS pages should never request a seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key.

Transparent infrastructure

Issuer identity, public transactions, market structure and ledger evidence should remain independently inspectable.

Voluntary witness

Prayer, testimony, NFT, trading and public social actions require deliberate user choice; passive browsing should remain available.

Stewardship over hype

JCS is not a promise of profit. Utility, clarity, safety, responsible disclosure and service take priority over promotional claims.

“And the Word became code, and the code dwelt among us.”Creative JCS project language. It is not a quotation from Scripture and is not a replacement for Scripture, prayer, church, pastoral care or faith in Jesus Christ.

Ethical access principles

The project direction rejects financial or engagement mechanisms that would turn spiritual participation into a paywall, popularity contest or urgency loop.

Human dignity boundary

No pay-to-pray

Basic prayer participation, Scripture access and safety resources should not require a token purchase.

No pay-to-be-heard

Money or token balances should not buy spiritual priority, crisis priority or doctrinal authority.

No token-weighted theology

Wallet balances do not determine truth, faithfulness, salvation, religious leadership or personal dignity.

No outrage engine

The long-term social direction should favor finite, meaningful interactions over compulsive scrolling, artificial urgency and engagement extraction.

Optional utility stays optional

Future token utility may support evidence, customization, creator tools or reusable access, but not basic spiritual participation or emergency resources.

Quiet and Sabbath design

The platform should be able to tell people that they have done enough for the day and help them leave the screen.

Safety for people whose faith may carry risk

Public blockchain transparency can conflict with personal safety. JCS therefore treats location restraint, silent access and user-controlled disclosure as core product requirements rather than optional polish.

Safety before publicity

Do not infer physical location

Wallets, nodes, relays, browsers, Xaman devices, transaction routes, prayer text and public keys must not be interpreted as a person's physical location. Prayer Map labels describe a prayer focus, not where the signer lives.

No public extraction routes

The public map must not publish escape routes, safe-house locations, movement plans or individual travel paths. Thresholded lines represent aggregate support relationships only.

Private Capsule is local-first

Private Capsule can encrypt a limited message locally, but it is not a guarantee against malware, hostile extensions, device compromise, physical coercion or mistakes in key handling.

Public XRPL records persist

A wallet address, transaction, memo, NFT or public registry entry can remain visible even if the website later changes. JCS cannot privately erase a validated XRPL transaction.

High-risk boundary: JCS is not an evacuation service, clandestine-operations platform, emergency dispatcher or replacement for qualified high-risk digital-security assistance. People in immediate danger should avoid publishing exact locations, routes, safe houses, identity documents or operational plans through a public blockchain workflow.

Where the project is headed

The current deployment is primarily a static client-side foundation. A durable Christian social network requires separate backend and trust infrastructure. These are design goals, not guaranteed features or delivery dates.
