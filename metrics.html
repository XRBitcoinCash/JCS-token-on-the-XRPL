Safety objective · measurable restraint

Privacy and communications boundaries

Some of the most important project metrics are intentionally absences: data that the architecture refuses to centralize or infer.

Exact location inference

0 accepted fields. No GPS, IP, browser, Xaman device, relay hostname or transaction route is treated as a person’s physical location.

Prayer Map release thresholds

Broad region: 5 distinct wallets. Country: 10. Extreme-pressure country: 20. The metrics page applies the same cohort rules.

Private Capsule measurement

Plaintext, keys and private delivery success are intentionally not centrally measurable. Only a user-chosen neutral receipt can become public.

Wallet minimization

Optional wallet inspection is JCS-scoped. Unrelated trust lines, NFTs and holdings are not listed or scored.

Map versus identity

Prayer-focus labels describe what a prayer concerns. They are not evidence that a signer lives in, traveled through or is physically present in that area.

No spiritual KPI

The page does not rank prayer effectiveness, religious worth, salvation, doctrine or human urgency using blockchain activity.

Interpretation workflow

Observe first. Derive carefully. Keep external context separate.

1 · Validate the source

Ledger facts come from validated XRPL responses. A disconnected or stale source is labeled rather than silently replaced with a favorable value.

2 · Preserve scope

Communication records, token obligations, AMM reserves and order books answer different questions. They are not merged into a single success score.

3 · Review the limitation

Market liquidity can change immediately. Communication counts cannot establish identity, truth, spiritual effect or safety. External providers have their own terms and data quality.

Security, market and interpretation notice

Currency symbols are not unique on XRPL. Verify the full JCS issuer before interacting. The Metrics page is informational and creates no Xaman transaction payloads. Public XRPL records are durable, but live server responses, AMM reserves, order books, account flags and trust-line counts can change. External charts can be delayed or unavailable. Metrics do not constitute financial advice, investment ratings, legal identity verification, a guarantee of liquidity, proof of prayer effectiveness, proof of persecution status, or proof of real-world location.
