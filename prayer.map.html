      Location and privacy rules
      • Existing NFT and registry records without a structured focus remain visible in totals as “map focus not published.”
      • No location is inferred from an XRPL node, validator, wallet, transaction route, name, prayer text, IP address, browser, or Xaman device.
      • JCS V6 records may contain one voluntary prayer-focus or support-focus country/region in signed public memos. It describes what the record concerns, not where the signer is.
      • Broad regions require at least 5 distinct signing accounts; countries require 10, and extreme-pressure countries require 20. Connections require qualifying source and destination groups, distinct parent prayers, and distinct responders.
      • Published area and connection totals are rounded down to five-record bands so a live refresh does not identify a single new contribution.
      • Support curves are descriptive aggregate relationships only. They must never be interpreted as travel paths, extraction routes, safe corridors, movement plans, or transaction routing.
      • These thresholds reduce casual exposure but cannot make public ledger activity anonymous. The map never uses a mileage-radius or synthetic personal coordinate.
      • Public circles require the applicable distinct-account threshold. Wallets, transaction hashes, NFT owners, exact times, and prayer text are never shown on this map.
      • The connected verification form asks for the prayer’s broad focus—not a vulnerable person’s physical location.
      How the XRPL reader works
      • Scans validated account_tx history for the JCS collector rPU6…RyYKd.
      • Accepts JCS V1–V6 public post registry payments and parses only allowlisted memo fields.
      • Resolves V6 community replies to parent prayer records, then releases only aggregate links that satisfy both endpoint and diversity thresholds.
      • Recognizes the current prayer NFT taxon 1246974803 and verifies referenced mint transactions when live transport permits.
      • Uses four public WebSocket endpoints in sequence, then the existing read-only Render proxy as a fallback.
      • Replaces the embedded audited snapshot only if the immutable ledger totals do not regress.
      Evidence limits
      • WWL is Christian-specific advocacy research. Scores measure pressure in five life spheres plus violence; compare scores, not rank movement alone.
      • USCIRF recommendations cover freedom of religion or belief for all faiths and are not a Christian-persecution score.
      • GDELT is automated multilingual media analysis. Its locations are places mentioned near the fixed query, not verified incident locations. Translation, geocoding, duplicates, media access, and false positives affect results.
      • “No comparable data,” “zero records,” “cached,” and “unavailable” are intentionally different states.
