    Geography and privacy limits

    Geographic interpretation: Map placement reflects a declared, voluntarily selected, or metadata-associated region. It does not verify an issuer's physical location, identity, or jurisdiction. XRPL AMMs are ledger objects and have no physical location.

    Prayer activity is accepted only as coarse aggregated cells. Precise locations, wallet identifiers, full prayer text, IP-derived location, and private information are not displayed.

    The browser requests the dark basemap from CARTO using OpenStreetMap data and loads pinned map libraries from unpkg. Those independent services receive ordinary network metadata such as IP address. Viewport bounds are sent to the map proxy only when its compatible capability manifest explicitly enables viewport-scoped queries.

    Indicators summarize available ledger and metadata signals. Missing data is not evidence of misconduct, and no status guarantees safety, value, identity, or legal standing.
